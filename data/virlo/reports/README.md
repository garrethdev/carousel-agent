## Anti-aging skincare

**In anti-aging, visual proof beats claims: before/after (lift 1.75), direct-address (lift 1.58) and relatable-scenario (lift 1.32) hooks built around one hero ingredient — retinol — massively outperform the saturated bold-claim/tutorial posts that make up most of the niche.**

- Before/after framing is the single highest-lift hook type in the niche _(before_after: 12% of all but 21% of top tier (lift 1.75), median 338,707 views vs 141,411 dataset median. Named winners: 'Me before using retinol:' (4,165,965), 'My skin while using retinol every day' (1,676,221), 'Tired eyes Before Dark circles using the Abib Collagen Eye Cream...' (1,018,053), 'here's my 3 weeks retinol journey (notice how my smile lines are almost gone)' (895,011 | 477.6x).)_
- Second-person 'this is your sign' direct address converts scroll into swipe _(direct_address: 7% of all, 11% of top tier (lift 1.58), median 254,903 views. Named winners: 'This is your sign to use retinol 🧖‍♀️✨💖' (892,417 | 51.9x), 'if you were looking for a sign to start using Retinol...' (682,260 | 13.6x), 'Rating Retinols as a medical aesthetician that works in derm' (254,903 | 181.9x).)_
- Relatable-scenario / cultural-commentary hooks deliver the highest median views of any type _(relatable_scenario: 4% of all, 5% of top tier (lift 1.32) but median 614,352 views — the highest median in the lift table. Includes the dataset max: 'young girls getting a 10-step skincare routine, anti aging products...trying to be older' (17,956,543 | 5677.1x, from a 3,163-follower account) and 'just when you want to give up there will be someone who will tell you to use Retinol' (614,352).)_
